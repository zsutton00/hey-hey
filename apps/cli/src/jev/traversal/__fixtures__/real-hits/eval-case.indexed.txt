Discover the governing eval design when asked to automatically retry failed attempts

situation:
A user wants the eval runner to automatically retry failed attempts and use a successful retry for comparison. The current @[design_doc:8a7aed78-e688-4d4e-8fc4-8b58c36df034|In-App Agent Evaluations], discoverable through its owning feature @[task:24aa8d29-3219-4d0e-b3dc-62ffdc7d1fce|Agent Evaluations], explicitly says the runner does not rescue stuck or failing attempts: intervention changes the conditions being measured, errors remain recorded against their attempts, independent attempts continue, and rerunning is a new attempt the user chooses. Automatic retries are outside the release scope. The request does not name the document. This case tests whether Project Assistant discovers the governing document and carries the conflict into its research handoff.
