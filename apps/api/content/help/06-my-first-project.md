Completing onboarding is just the first step in building your project.

Hey Hey gives you an initial structure based on what it learned about your product, your users, and the way your team works. That structure is a starting point, not a final answer. As you make decisions, complete work, and learn more about the product, your project should evolve with you.

This guide walks through the first useful cycle: improve your feature tree, turn product intent into concrete work, use a plan for larger changes, choose the right coding-session approach, preserve what you learn, and give Hey Hey the right amount of context.

You do not need to model everything perfectly before beginning. Start with one real outcome, use the project while you work, and improve its structure as your understanding grows.
